Locker is a tiny app with no user interface of its own that does exactly one
thing: lock the screen the instant it's launched.

It's meant to be set as the target app for Pixel's "Quick Tap" gesture
(double-tap the back of the phone -> "Open app"): a double tap on the back
of the phone then locks the screen right away, no detour through settings
or quick settings needed.

Unlike the lock triggered by a device-admin app, Locker does not mark the
lock as "administrator locked" - fingerprint and face unlock keep working
normally afterwards.

How it works:

* The app has no visible UI. On launch it immediately locks the screen and
  closes itself again.
* Locking the screen requires an accessibility service, since only such a
  service is allowed to call the underlying system action. The service does
  not read screen content, does not draw any overlay, and does not react to
  anything besides the app being launched.
* On first launch, you're asked once to enable the service in the
  accessibility settings.

There is no internet access, no ads, no analytics/tracking, and no
dependencies beyond the Android platform itself.
